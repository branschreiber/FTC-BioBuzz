Model accuracy notes

What is dimensioned versus schematic.

The manual specifies a 12 ft × 12 ft field in the tile layout shown as six 24 in. tiles per side; the HIVE is centered and its frame base is approximately 49.46 × 38.95 in. Four FLOWERS attach to the perimeter. Their displayed locations are traced approximately from the manual's field diagram, while exact setup should be checked against official CAD. This is a planning model, not a certified field drawing.

Manual dimensions
Field tiles: 24 × 24 in nominal, 6 × 6 layout. HIVE frame base: 49.46 × 38.95 in. CELL opening: approximately 20 in wide × 14 in high × 12 in deep. FLOWER top opening: approximately 4 in diameter; lower ring approx. 2.79 in diameter. FLOWER footprint symbol is schematic.
The HIVE and FLOWER positions are modeled from the manual's field illustration. The drawing is a planning reference; use official field CAD for precise fabrication, inspection, or event setup.
